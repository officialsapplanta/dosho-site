Earn on Dosho

Two ways to earn.

Creators

Get paid for your time

Any verified user can be a creator. Set your own rate per message and per minute of video, host rooms, and receive gifts. Earnings are paid out to the payout method you add in the app.

Example: 100 coins a minute × a 10 minute call = 1,000 coins earned
Affiliate

Share your code, earn for life

Everyone is automatically an affiliate. Your username is your referral code, and you earn 5% of whatever the people who join through you spend or earn. It's not a one-time bonus.

Example: they spend 1,000 coins → you earn 50, every time

Trust & safety

Built on trust.

Real identity verification

Creators confirm their age and identity with a government ID and a live selfie before they can earn.

18+ only

Every account confirms it meets our age requirement at sign-up.

Server-side wallet

Balances and charges are calculated on our servers, not your device, so a balance can't be spoofed or spent twice.

Screenshots alert both sides

Private chats and rooms tell everyone when someone tries to capture the screen. See everything we do.
