How it works

Watch, create, connect.

Live

Lives and PK battles

Watch hosts from around the world, join the live chat, and cheer for your favourite in a PK battle. Go live yourself in a few taps, with a beauty filter.

Reels

Short videos with music

Scroll reels picked for you, search hashtags, and make your own: record in the app, trim, add a look, text and music, or make a duet or a stitch.

Gifts

Show some love

Send animated gifts that light up the whole screen. Coins are optional and you only buy what you want. Level up, check in daily and collect avatar frames.

For hosts

Two ways to earn.

Hosts

Earn from the gifts you get

Go live, grow your audience and earn from the gifts viewers send you. Level up as a host with daily tasks and contests, and withdraw your earnings to M-Pesa or PayPal.

Only earned coins can be withdrawn. Coins you buy stay in the app.
Affiliate

Share your code, earn for life

Everyone is automatically an affiliate. Your username is your referral code, and you earn 5% of what the people who join through you earn. It's not a one-time bonus.
