Trust & safety

Private by design.

Closeness needs safety. Here is what protects you on Dosho today, and the one thing we are still working on.

Protected today

Screenshots alert both sides

On Android, chats and rooms block screenshots and screen recording outright. On iPhone a capture cannot be blocked, so the other person is told the moment it happens, and so are you.

Your coordinates stay private

Your precise location is used to suggest people and rooms near you. Nobody is ever shown your exact coordinates.

Verified creators

Creators confirm their age and identity with an ID and a live selfie. Those photos are used only for verification and are deleted once they are no longer needed.

A wallet that cannot be spoofed

Balances and charges are worked out on our servers, not on your phone, so they cannot be edited or spent twice.

Deleting your account means it

When you delete your account your profile disappears straight away, and after 30 days your remaining personal details are permanently anonymized.

AI only if both of you say yes

AI chat summaries are off by default. A chat can only be summarized when both people have turned the feature on, and only when one of you asks. The text of the chat is sent to an AI service to write the summary, which is saved only to the private Journal of the person who asked.

On our roadmap

End-to-end encryption Not yet

Messages travel over encrypted connections, but they are not end-to-end encrypted yet, which means Dosho's systems can access them, for example to handle a report. We want to change that, so not even we can read them, and we will announce it here when it ships.
