01 Our policy

Dosho has zero tolerance for child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE) in any form. This applies to every part of the platform — profiles, messages, calls, and Rooms — with no exceptions.

Dosho is restricted to users 18 and older. Content involving, sexualizing, or endangering minors is never permitted, and any account found to violate this is terminated immediately and permanently.

This standard is also published in our Community Guidelines (§1, Zero-tolerance content) and Privacy Policy.

02 Prevention

Dosho takes the following steps before and during account use to keep minors off the platform:

  • Age gate at signup. Every account must confirm a date of birth establishing they are 18 or older before an account can be created.
  • Identity verification. Creators go through a document- and liveness-based identity verification process (government ID capture, face match, and a guided liveness check) before their profile can go live, reducing the risk of falsified ages or impersonation.
  • No open discovery of minors. Profiles and matches are only surfaced between verified adult accounts.

03 Detection & reporting

Every profile, message, and call includes an in-app way to report or block instantly. Reports involving suspected CSAE are treated as the highest priority and escalated immediately, ahead of Dosho's standard moderation queue.

Anyone — a user, a parent, or a member of the public — can report a concern about child safety on Dosho by emailing the contact below, without needing an account.

04 Our response

When a report or internal review confirms a CSAE violation, Dosho:

  • Removes the offending content immediately.
  • Permanently terminates the account, with no refund of any remaining coin balance.
  • Preserves relevant account and content data as evidence.
  • Reports the incident to the National Center for Missing & Exploited Children (NCMEC) and/or law enforcement as required by applicable law.

This mirrors the enforcement standard published in our Community Guidelines (§5).
